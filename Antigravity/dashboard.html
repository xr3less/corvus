Setup Guide 1 / 2

Step 1: Create a Discord Application

Go to the Discord Developer Portal and create a new application.

1
Open Discord Developer Portal Open Developer Portal
2
Click "New Application"

Top right corner of the Developer Portal. Name your bot and click "Create".

Tip: Make sure you're logged into Discord with the account you want to own this bot.
Visual Reference
Applications + New Application

Your apps will appear here...

Step 2: Configure Your Application

Enable required permissions and copy your bot token.

1
Enable Privileged Gateway Intents

On the Bot page, scroll to "Privileged Gateway Intents" and enable all three toggles: Presence Intent, Server Members Intent, and Message Content Intent.

2
Click "Save Changes"

The green button at the bottom of the Bot page.

3
Copy your bot token

Scroll up to the TOKEN section, click "Reset Token" to reveal it, then copy it.

Your token is encrypted locally and never shared.
