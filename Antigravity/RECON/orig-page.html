FAQ

Good questions, answered.

What's free, and what needs Pro?
The whole studio is open to explore with no account needed to start. Exporting needs a free account: free export goes up to Preview size: clean (no watermark) for PNG, watermarked for video. Pryzm Pro is a subscription that unlocks Small, HD, Full HD, 2K, and 4K output, removes the video watermark, and adds longer loops and higher frame rates.
Can I use what I make commercially?
Absolutely. Backgrounds and loops you export are yours for personal and commercial projects. You just can't resell them as standalone stock.
Where do my images go?
Rendering happens entirely in your browser, so nothing leaves your device while you design. When you save a look, that look (including any photo you added and a preview image) is stored privately in your account so you can reopen and keep editing it. Only you can see your saved looks, unless you choose to share one.
Can I share a look with a teammate?
Yes. Save it, press Share and turn on Allow remixing: you get a link that opens the look, or a Lab stack or Flow project, in their editor as a copy of their own, photo included. Your original is never touched, the link always shows your latest save, and turning the switch off stops the link at once.
What video formats can I export?
MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP9), and AV1, all as seamless loops. Free video exports are watermarked and at Preview size; Pro removes the watermark and unlocks Small, HD, Full HD, 2K, and 4K.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Pryzm runs in the browser with real-time WebGL. Just open the studio and start.
How does Pryzm Pro billing work?
Pryzm Pro is a subscription billed through Polar, monthly or yearly for three months free. Sign in, subscribe, and Pro unlocks on your account across devices. Cancel anytime, and you keep access through the end of the period you've paid for.
