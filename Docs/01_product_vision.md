# 01 — Product Vision

## Status: DRAFT (filled 2026-09-07, pending founder read)

> What we're building and why. The "north star." Locked direction per D-003/D-006/D-008.

---

## 1. The one-sentence product

Corvus is an AI Discord-bot builder that lets non-coder server owners describe the bot they want in plain English and get a live, Corvus-hosted bot that's theirs to run and manage — no code, no token paste, no second server needed.

---

## 2. The problem

Small/medium Discord owners run communities on 4–5 rented bots (MEE6, Dyno, Carl-bot, Ticket Tool, Jockie) with 4–5 dashboards, per-server paywalls ($12/server/mo stacks), and setup hell (role hierarchy, intents, token paste) they cannot diagnose. Evinced: 31 buyer voices ranked paywall-creep / sprawl / setup-hell top (see `Marketing/vibebot-deep-research-2026-09-07.md §5`). Today they cope by paying per-server, learning 4 permission models, or giving up customization entirely.

---

## 3. Who it's for

Non-coder small/medium server owners (gaming, streamer, friend/study groups, ~50–5,000 members) who feel every outage personally. Big-server admins with devs can buy Studio later but are NOT the V1 design center. (Locked D-008; detailed in `02_strategy.md`.)

---

## 4. The core value

A bot that is YOURS and WORKS: described in words, live in minutes, managed from one dashboard, never leaking data on restart, never billing you for our failures. Everything else (marketplace, analytics depth, music) is secondary.

---

## 5. What it is NOT (scope guard)

No in-server setup (panel-only, privacy). No unlimited-servers tier until abuse-tested. No ready-made music template until legal read. No full in-Discord settings (thin status/link/log only). No global slash commands default. No native mobile app. No node-graph editor. No template revenue-share without curation. (Locked D-006/D-009/D-015; Never-list in `Marketing/corvus-model-and-pricing-2026-09-07.md §6`.)

---

## 6. What success looks like

A stranger connects a server, interviews in the panel, installs a template, describes one diff, simulates, publishes to a test guild, and wakes a live bot — without opening docs, pasting a token, or paying before day 4. Then trial→paid conversion funds the next 100 bots at ~€25/mo infra. (Numbers in `02_strategy.md` and `03_validation_plan.md`.)
